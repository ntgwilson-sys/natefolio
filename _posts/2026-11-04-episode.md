a seven track concept record about being there and not being there at the same time. bloghaus and experimental house, with midwest emo creeping in round the edges.

the whole thing runs like a pressure curve across the tracklist, so i built the rollout the same way. a diy campaign across instagram, tiktok, bandcamp and soundcloud, leaning on doubled vocals, raw against processed, and pronouns that never quite sit still.
